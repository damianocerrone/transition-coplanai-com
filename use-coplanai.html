Subscriber

A subscription to the platform. Your team runs its own collaborative planning workshops, when it needs them.

Who it is for

Step 4 · Strategic partnership

Governance Transformation

Collaborative planning leaves the one-off workshop and becomes part of how the institution works, across the departments that shape a place.

Who it is for
Governments, cities and international organisations whose leaders want public imagination to be a standing part of how decisions are made.
What is redesigned
The institution’s internal processes, its governance model and its culture, around collaborative planning with AI.
What changes
  • Where the public enters. People come in earlier, while the options are still open.
  • Your statutory and consultation moments, with collaborative planning built into them.
  • Work across departments. Teams inside the institution work on the same scenarios and evaluations as the public.
  • Governance, data and accountability that stand up to public scrutiny.
  • A long-term partnership while the practice becomes standard.

The platform

One engagement, three surfaces, one record.

The AI does two jobs. It generates scenarios from people’s own words, on the real place. And it evaluates them, against the institution’s own policy, design guides and law, and for their likely environmental and social impact. People deliberate; the institution decides.

AI governance · limited risk
