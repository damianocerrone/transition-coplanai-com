Humanising Cities, Oman

Imagine · Oman Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning · 2024

At the Royal Academy of Management in Muscat, around 25 governors from across Oman’s regions took part in a capacity-building workshop combining a Rotterdam urban-transformation case study, Humankind’s Good Public Space Model, and a hands-on AI-assisted redesign of selected Omani public spaces — with an AI tool provided inside the Humankind-led session.

Who took part

Around 25 regional governors, convened by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Planning.

What the tool did

AI-generated redesigns of selected Omani public spaces, applying the Good Public Space Model in a guided exercise for senior decision-makers.
